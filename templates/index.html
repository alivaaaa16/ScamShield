        Best Practices

        Four Rules to Avoid Online Scams

        Keep these principles in mind whenever dealing with unfamiliar messages or alerts.

        Rule 1

        Never Trust Artificial Urgency

        Scammers manufacture panic ("Blocked in 2 hours!", "Arrest warrant issued") to override your critical thinking. Real banks give formal notice.

        Rule 2

        Verify via Verified Channels

        Never call the phone number in the SMS or click their link. Turn your bank card over and dial the official phone number printed on the plastic.

        Rule 3

        OTPs and PINs Are Strictly Secret

        No legitimate bank, courier, or tech company employee will ever ask you to read out a One-Time Password (OTP) or authentication code.

        Rule 4

        Inspect Links Carefully

        Scammers often use lookalike domains (e.g., bank-verify-help.com instead of bank.com). When in doubt, type the official URL yourself.
